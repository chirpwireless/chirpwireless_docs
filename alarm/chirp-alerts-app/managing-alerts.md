# Managing Alerts

The Chirp Alerts app has two tabs for managing your alerts and notification rules directly from your phone.

## Inbox

The Inbox tab shows all alerts for your currently selected home, with the most recent at the top.

Each alert shows:
- **Alert title and message** — what triggered the alert
- **Timestamp** — when it happened
- **Status indicator** — a red dot means the alert is still active; a green dot means it has been resolved

The inbox refreshes automatically while you have the app open, so new alerts appear without pulling to refresh.

### Resolving an Alert

Tap any active alert in the inbox to resolve it. A confirmation appears before the alert is marked as resolved in Chirp. Once resolved, the status changes to green and other household members see it as resolved too.

This is the same as clicking "Dismiss & Acknowledge" on the alarm screen — it resolves the alert for everyone, not just on your phone.

### Deleting an Alert

Swipe left on any alert to delete it. A confirmation dialog appears before the alert is permanently removed.

## Rules

The Rules tab shows your notification rules — these are the alarm definitions you created on the Chirp web platform that have push as a delivery channel.

Each rule shows its name and a **toggle** to enable or disable it. If a rule is generating too many notifications, you can turn it off directly from your phone without opening the web platform.

If the Rules tab shows "No rules configured," it means no notification rules have been created yet. Rules are created on the web platform — see [Set Up a Home Alert](../set-up-a-home-alert.md) to create your first one.

## Resolving vs Closing

There are two ways to respond to a critical alert, and they mean different things:

| Action | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Close** | Silences the alarm sound on your phone only. The alert stays active in Chirp. Escalation continues if configured. | Alarm screen |
| **Dismiss & Acknowledge** | Silences the alarm AND resolves the alert in Chirp for everyone. | Alarm screen |
| **Tap to resolve** | Resolves the alert in Chirp for everyone. | Inbox |
| **Swipe to delete** | Permanently removes the alert from your inbox. | Inbox |

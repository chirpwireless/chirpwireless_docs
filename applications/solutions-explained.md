# What Is a Ready-Made Home Solution?

Two words sit side by side in Chirp, and they describe different things. Knowing which is which makes every screen in this section easier to read.

## How is a solution different from an application?

Think of a family recipe and the dinner you cook from it.

* An **application** is the dinner on your table: a working household setup inside your Chirp organization. You can rename it, add sensors, redraw the dashboard, or retire it whenever you like.
* A **solution** is the recipe card a provider hands out: a complete setup that someone has already figured out. Installing it cooks you a dinner of your own.

When you install a solution you receive an ordinary application. Nothing in the application list marks it as installed from a solution, and nothing is synced back and forth afterward. Edit your copy, rename it, or delete it, and the provider's solution and every other household's copy stay exactly as they were. If a provider later withdraws a solution, its catalog card disappears, but the application you already created keeps running.

## What comes with a solution?

| Piece | What you will see in your home |
|---|---|
| Practice sensors | Emulated devices with named readings, each marked with an **Emulator** badge. |
| Automations | Rules that watch those readings, already deployed and running. |
| Alerts | Alarm definitions whose recipient is you, the person who pressed install. |
| Dashboard | A finished view in which every widget is already wired to a practice sensor. |

## Where do I find solutions in Chirp?

Choose **Applications** in the left sidebar. The page is called **Applications and solutions** and has tabs across the top:

* **Solutions** is the first tab. It is the catalog of setups your provider has published.
* **My applications** lists every application in your organization, both ones you built and ones you installed.

Some providers also have a **Use cases** tab. It is a management screen for the people who publish solutions, and you can ignore it as a household member.

## Who prepares solutions?

Providers are the businesses that supply and set up equipment, such as home-security or leak-protection specialists. They build a setup once and share it, so households never begin from a blank screen. You see the solutions that your own provider has published, and the collection keeps growing.

## What is a use case?

A **use case** is a short description of the problem a solution is meant to solve, such as watching a holiday home or catching a leak early. Providers use use cases to arrange their catalog by need instead of by product. You never have to manage them to benefit from them.

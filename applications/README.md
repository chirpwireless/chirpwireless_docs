# Applications

An **application** is a place to bring together the parts of a household setup. It can hold the sensors you watch, the dashboards you check, the rules that respond to readings, and the alarm definitions that decide how you hear about a problem.

The longer-term aim is for equipment providers and dealers to prepare complete solutions that you can select and deploy. That experience is planned. This first release provides the foundation by letting you configure and organize the parts of your own setup.

Imagine a **Home Watch** application for keeping an eye on your home while you are away. Its dashboard shows useful readings, its devices provide those readings, and its rules and alarms handle the situations that need attention. You can find those pieces together instead of remembering where you configured each one.

## What goes into an application?

**Devices** provide measurements or states, such as temperature or whether a door is open. **Dashboards** turn that information into a view you can check. **Rules** decide when something should happen. **Alarms** define the message and notification behavior a rule uses to tell you about it.

You choose and configure these resources yourself. Adding a sensor to an application does not automatically create a dashboard or an alert for it.

## Find your household setup

Choose **Applications** in the sidebar, then open an application under **My applications**. The **Dashboard** tab is for viewing its dashboards. **Content** shows the associated devices, dashboards, rules, and alarms.

The application belongs to your selected organization. Family members still need the relevant permissions to see or change its contents; creating an application does not grant anyone extra access.

## Build one useful setup first

Start by [creating Home Watch](creating-an-application.md), then [add the resources](organizing-content.md) you need. A smaller application that solves one problem is easier to understand than an empty collection of unrelated devices. You might later make another for garden care or a holiday home.

## What this foundation makes possible

Applications are a first step toward ready-to-use solutions supplied by specialists. For example, a provider of home-protection equipment could prepare a leak-monitoring template with the sensors, a useful dashboard, and the rules and alerts already arranged. A household would choose the prepared setup and deploy it, instead of configuring every piece independently. Other specialists could prepare solutions for different equipment or needs.

That template and dealer experience is planned. Today you can build your own household solution and keep its devices, dashboards, rules, and alarms together in Applications.

# How do I explore ready-made solutions?

A preview lets you look through a finished home setup before you commit to anything. The flow is short: press **Preview**, then **Use this solution**, then **Install solution** on the next screen. This guide takes you through the catalog and the preview, one screen at a time.

## How do I open the catalog?

1. Choose **Applications** in the left sidebar. The page title reads **Applications and solutions**.
2. Select the **Solutions** tab (the first tab, next to **My applications**).

Under the **Solutions** heading you will read a short paragraph about what a solution contains. In plain words: practice sensors, automations, alerts, and a ready dashboard. The practice sensors send sample readings, so the dashboard is alive the moment you install and you need no hardware. (A connector is the link that brings a sensor's readings into Chirp. Practice sensors use one called the Emulator connector.)

Chirp controls the catalog. What you find there depends on what has been added so far.

If the grid is empty, the page says **Your provider has not published any solutions yet**. That is the exact message, and it only means nothing has been added to the catalog yet. Check back later. If the list cannot be fetched, you will see **Couldn't load the solution catalog**. Reload the page after a moment.

## What is on a solution card?

Each solution is one card in the grid. From top to bottom a card holds:

1. **A photo** chosen for the application (some cards have none).
2. **The name** of the setup.
3. **A short description.**
4. **Four small counters:** virtual devices (the practice sensors), rules (the automations), widgets (the tiles on the dashboard), and alarms. They tell you at a glance whether a card is a small helper or a setup for the whole house.
5. **A Preview button** at the bottom. Press it to look inside.

## How do I look inside a solution without installing it?

Press **Preview** on a card. Nothing is created in your home while you browse.

The preview page has a back arrow and the solution's name in the top-left corner, its description underneath, and a **Use this solution** button in the top-right corner. Below that, two tabs switch the view:

* **Dashboard** shows the finished dashboard with readings from the practice sensors. Look around like on any dashboard. You cannot change anything here.
* **Content** explains the setup in words, in these blocks:
  * **About** the setup, with two columns beside it: **Who it's for** and **How it works right now**. Many applications put an installation checklist in the second column, naming where each real sensor goes.
  * **Virtual devices**, each row carrying an **Emulator** badge. These are the practice sensors.
  * **Rules**, with the sentence that each rule describes.
  * **Alarms**, each with a severity label such as Low or Critical. An alarm is a notification that tells you something needs attention.
  * An **Emulator** card. It says you can swap the practice sensors for real ones later, and that your devices, mappings, rules, and widgets stay as they are.

## What if the preview shows a message?

* **Preview is unavailable right now:** try again shortly.
* **This solution has no dashboards to preview:** the setup has no dashboard, but **Content** still works.
* **Solution not found:** Chirp may have removed it from the catalog since you opened the page.

When you like what you see, press **Use this solution** to open the install page. There you press **Install solution**. Continue with [Installing a Ready-Made Solution](installing-a-ready-made-solution.md).

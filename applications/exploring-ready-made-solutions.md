# How do I explore ready-made solutions?

This page shows you how to look through the solutions catalog and peek inside one. Nothing is created in your home while you browse.

The path is short: press **Preview**, then **Use this solution**, then **Install solution** on the next screen. First, the catalog.

## How do I open the catalog?

1. Choose **Applications** in the left sidebar. The page title reads **Applications and solutions**.
2. Select the **Solutions** tab. It is the first tab, next to **My applications**.

Under the **Solutions** heading, a short paragraph tells you what a solution contains: practice sensors, automations, alerts, and a ready dashboard. The practice sensors send sample readings, so the dashboard is alive right after you install and you need no hardware. They connect through the Emulator connector, the link that brings their readings into Chirp.

Not sure what a solution is? [Start here](solutions-explained.md).

Chirp keeps the catalog, so what you see depends on what has been added so far.

## What if the catalog is empty?

If the grid is empty, the page says **Your provider has not published any solutions yet**. That is the exact message. It only means nothing has been added yet, so check back later.

If the list cannot be fetched, you see **Couldn't load the solution catalog**. Reload the page after a moment.

## What is on a solution card?

Each solution is one card in the grid. From top to bottom, a card holds:

1. **A photo** chosen for the application. Some cards have none.
2. **The name** of the setup.
3. **A short description.**
4. **Four small counters:** virtual devices (the practice sensors), rules (the automations), widgets (the tiles on the dashboard), and alarms.
5. **A Preview button** at the bottom.

The counters tell you at a glance whether a card is a small helper or a setup for the whole house.

## How do I look inside a solution without installing it?

Press **Preview** on a card. The preview page has a back arrow and the solution's name in the top-left corner, with its description underneath. In the top-right corner you find the **Use this solution** button.

Below that, two tabs switch the view.

### What does the Dashboard tab show?

**Dashboard** shows the finished dashboard with readings from the practice sensors. Look around as you would on any dashboard. You cannot change anything here.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/chirp-solutions-preview-dashboard.jpg" alt="The solution preview page with the solution name, a Use this solution button, the Dashboard and Content tabs, and a live widget showing a practice sensor reading"><figcaption><p>The Dashboard tab of a preview.</p></figcaption></figure>

### What does the Content tab show?

**Content** describes the setup in plain words. It has these blocks:

* **About** the setup, with two columns beside it: **Who it's for** and **How it works right now**. Many solutions put an installation checklist in the second column, naming where each real sensor goes.
* **Virtual devices**, each row carrying an **Emulator** badge. These are the practice sensors.
* **Rules**, with the sentence that each rule describes.
* **Alarms**, each with a severity label such as Low or Critical. An alarm is a notification that tells you something needs attention.
* An **Emulator** card. It says you can swap the practice sensors for real ones later, and that your devices, mappings, rules, and widgets stay as they are.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/chirp-solutions-preview-content.jpg" alt="The Content tab of a solution preview with the About card, Virtual devices with an Emulator badge, Rules, Alarms and the Emulator explanation"><figcaption><p>The Content tab of a preview.</p></figcaption></figure>

## What if the preview shows a message?

* **Preview is unavailable right now:** try again shortly.
* **This solution has no dashboards to preview:** the setup has no dashboard, but **Content** still works.
* **Solution not found:** Chirp may have removed it from the catalog since you opened the page.

## What do I do when I like a solution?

Press **Use this solution** to open the install page, then **Install solution** there. [Installing a ready-made solution](installing-a-ready-made-solution.md) walks you through it.

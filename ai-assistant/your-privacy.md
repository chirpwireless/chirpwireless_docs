# Your Privacy

A question to the Chirp AI Assistant can include information about your home. For example, asking when a door last opened may send the sensor's name and recent readings to the AI model used to answer. Your messages, answers and retrieved information can also appear in conversation history.

## Choose the account and access

The built-in assistant uses your login and the selected home or organization for its requests. Check the members and permissions you have set. Read any **Confirm Action** prompt before approving a change; choose **Cancel** if it is not what you want. Routine setup can proceed without a separate prompt.

If you see information or a proposed action outside the access you expect, stop that task and contact **support [at] chirpwireless.io** (replace `[at]` with `@`).

## Keep unnecessary private details out of chat

Anything you type can become part of the conversation. Sensor readings included in an answer or tool result can be saved too. Avoid account passwords, payment details and unrelated secrets. Use the dedicated settings for a model-provider key; [Your Chats and Your Own AI](chats-and-your-own-ai.md) explains those settings.

## Understand the provider you choose

The model provider runs the AI that produces the answer. Chirp's configured route uses OpenRouter to reach the selected model. If you use your own provider credentials or connect another AI application, review that provider's retention, model-training and location terms. They can differ between providers and settings.

A model endpoint you operate yourself changes where model requests go; it does not remove Chirp's own conversation handling. Disconnecting an external application also does not erase information it already received.

## Delete a conversation or request account closure

Use the chat-management instructions to delete an individual conversation. This is separate from closing an account. Account closure does not currently automate complete conversation deletion and removal of related copies across every system.

Email **support [at] chirpwireless.io** if you want personal information removed, an account closed or a result covering provider-held copies. Identify the account and requested outcome without resending private conversation contents. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting.

The response should explain what was removed and whether any records must remain for a specific legal reason. Backups can have a separate expiry cycle, and deleted information must not return to normal use after a restore. The [Privacy Policy](https://chirpwireless.io/privacy-policy/) explains your rights and the request process.

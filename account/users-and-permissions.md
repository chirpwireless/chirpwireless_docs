# Household Members

Your Chirp home works best when the people who live there can see what matters to them. Invite family members, housemates, or even a property manager -- then decide exactly what each person can view and change.

## Getting There

Click your avatar or name in the bottom-left corner to open the user menu, then select **Users**.

## The Members List

The page shows everyone who belongs to your home, plus any invitations that have not been accepted yet.

- **Search** -- Use the "Search users..." bar at the top to find a specific person by name or email.
- **Owner badge** -- The home owner is marked with an "Owner" label. The owner always has full access and cannot be removed from this page (ownership transfer happens in [Home Settings](organization-settings.md)).
- **Pending invitations** -- Invites that have not been accepted yet appear in the list with an info tooltip: "Awaiting invitation confirmation."

## Inviting Someone New

1. Click **+ Add user** in the top-right corner.
2. Enter the person's **email address**.
3. Set permissions for each section of Chirp individually. For every page -- Dashboards, Devices, Rules, Alerts, and so on -- choose one of three levels:
   - **Edit** -- Full access to view and make changes.
   - **View** -- Can see everything but cannot modify anything.
   - **No access** -- The section is hidden entirely for that person.
4. Send the invitation.

The person receives an email with a link to join. Until they accept, they appear as a pending invitation in the members list.

## Changing Someone's Permissions

Click the **Edit** button next to any member to reopen the permissions panel. Adjust individual sections as needed and save. Changes take effect the next time that person loads Chirp.

## Removing a Member

Click the **Remove** button next to the person you want to remove. A confirmation dialog appears before the removal goes through. Once removed, the person loses all access to your home's sensors, dashboards, automations, and alerts.

The home owner cannot be removed. If you need to change who owns the home, see [Home Settings](organization-settings.md).

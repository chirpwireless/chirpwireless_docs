# Switching Homes

Switching homes changes the Chirp workspace you are viewing. Each **home**, called an **organization** in the menu, has its own devices, automations, members, and permissions. Your personal login can belong to several of these workspaces.

For example, your house and a cabin might use separate workspaces. Rooms and location labels organize devices inside a workspace; choosing a room does not switch homes. Use **My organizations** when you need to work with a different home's data, and check the selected name before editing anything.

## Getting There

Click your avatar or name in the bottom-left corner to open the user menu. The **My organizations** section lists every home you belong to.

Each entry shows:

- An avatar with the first letter of the home's name.
- The home name.
- An **Owner** badge if you own that home.
- A **checkmark** next to the home you are currently viewing.

## How to Switch

Click any home in the list. Chirp instantly loads that home's context -- all dashboards, sensors, automations, and alerts update to reflect the selected home. Your choice is remembered, so the next time you open Chirp it returns to the last home you were in.

## Joining Another Home

You become a member of a new home by accepting an invitation from someone in that home. Once accepted, the home appears in your **My organizations** list and you can switch to it at any time.

If you only belong to one home, the list shows a single entry.

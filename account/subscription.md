# Subscription

**How to get here:** Click your name in the bottom-left corner of the screen, then select **Subscription**.

Your subscription determines how many sensors you can connect, how many dashboards you can create, how far back your data history reaches, and which notification channels are available.

## Choosing a plan

Chirp offers several plan tiers — from a free starter option to plans designed for larger homes or properties. Each plan card shows the price, billing interval (monthly or annual), and a feature comparison so you can see exactly what you get.

**If you're new:** The free tier lets you connect a limited number of sensors without entering payment details. When you're ready for more, pick a plan and tap **Start trial** to begin — you'll be taken to a secure checkout page to complete the setup.

**If you already have a plan:** Your current plan is marked in the comparison. To change plans, tap a different tier and follow the confirmation steps. To manage billing details, invoices, or cancel, tap **Manage my subscription** — this opens your billing portal where all payment management happens.

## What each plan includes

The plan comparison shows limits for:

- Number of connected sensors
- Number of custom dashboards
- Data history retention (how many days of sensor history you can query)
- Notification channels (email, SMS, push)
- Rules engine capacity
- Support level

## SMS top-up

If your plan supports SMS notifications, you can purchase additional SMS credits from this page. Enter the number of credits you need, see the cost per message, and complete the purchase. Credits are valid for one year from the date of purchase.

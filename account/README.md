# Account

Manage your household members, subscription, and home settings. These options are found in the **user menu** at the bottom-left corner of the screen — separate from Settings in the sidebar.

Click your name at the bottom-left to open the menu. From there you can:

- **Users** — invite household members and manage what each person can see or change
- **Subscription** — view your plan, upgrade, or manage billing
- **Organization settings** — rename your home or transfer ownership (owner only)
- **My organizations** — switch between homes if you belong to more than one

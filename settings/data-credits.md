# Data Credits

Data credits power everything you do on Chirp. They cover service fees, device operation, and other platform activity. You can check your balance and add more credits from **Settings > Data credits** in the sidebar.

## Your Balance

At the top of the page, a banner displays how many data credits you currently have. One data credit equals one US cent. Credits are non-refundable and non-transferable.

If you have questions about how credits work or how they are consumed, the banner includes a direct link to Help & FAQ.

## Adding Credits

Below the balance, the **Top Up Data Credit balance** section lets you purchase additional credits. Enter the number of credits you want to add -- the minimum per transaction is 500 credits. If you type a lower number, an error message reminds you of the minimum.

Three payment options are available:

- **PayPal** -- pay directly through your PayPal account.
- **Card** -- use a credit or debit card, processed through PayPal.
- **Crypto** -- pay with cryptocurrency. This option opens an external invoice page where you complete the transaction.

Pick the method that works best for you, and the credits appear on your balance once the payment is confirmed.

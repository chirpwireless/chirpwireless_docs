# Device Sharing

Want a neighbor, property manager, or family member to keep an eye on one of your sensors? Chirp lets you share individual sensors with other users so they can view the data without touching your settings. Head to **Settings > Device sharing** to see everything you have shared.

## Viewing Shared Sensors

The page shows a table with every sensor you have shared. Each row lists the sensor name, its type, and the email address of the person who has access. If you have not shared anything yet, you will see the message: *"You don't have any shared devices -- it will be visible here."*

## Removing Someone's Access

To stop sharing a sensor, click the trash icon in its row. Chirp asks you to confirm: *"Are you sure you want to unshare this device with user {email}?"* Click **Unshare** and that person loses access right away.

## How to Share a Sensor

Sharing starts from the sensor itself, not from this page. Open the sensor you want to share, go to its settings, type the recipient's email address, and tap **Share**. The person shows up in a list underneath, with an **X** button if you change your mind.

For the full walkthrough, see [Sensor Details](../devices/sensor-details.md).

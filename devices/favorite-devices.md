# Favorite Devices

The Favorite devices card on your Overview page gives you an at-a-glance view of the sensors and gateways you care about most. Each favorite shows its name, battery level (if available), and when it was last seen.

## How to favorite a gateway

Open a gateway's detail page by clicking its name in the **Gateways** list. On the gateway card, click the **star icon** to add it to your favorites.

- **Star filled** = favorited. Tooltip: "Remove from favorites."
- **Star outline** = not favorited. Tooltip: "Add to favorites."

Click the star again to unfavorite.

## Where favorites appear

Your favorites show up in the **Favorite devices** card on the Overview page. This gives you a quick health check for the gateways you want to monitor regularly, without navigating to their detail pages each time.

## Sensor favoriting

The favorites feature is evolving as Chirp transitions to the new sensor management interface. Sensor favoriting from sensor lists is not currently available in the new interface. To view and manage your sensors, use **Devices** in the sidebar or open a connection's sensor list.

## If you don't have any favorites yet

The Favorite devices card shows a helpful message: *"You don't have favorite devices"* with the hint *"Add the device to your favorites by clicking on the star in the device list."* (This hint text reflects an older interface. In the current sensor management interface, gateway favoriting is available from the gateway detail page.)

## Tips

- **Favorite the gateways you rely on.** The Overview page is the first thing you see when you open Chirp — make it show what matters most.
- **Keep it focused.** A short favorites list is more useful than a long one.
